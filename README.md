## 📖 About The Project
**Quorum** is a web-based team-formation and peer-reputation platform for university students. It solves two major problems in academic group projects:
1. **The Lone Ranger Problem:** Capable students with small social networks get left out of good groups.
2. **The Free Rider Problem:** No system exists to record who actually did the work, allowing free riders to go unpunished.

Quorum solves this by introducing a **Verification Layer**. Students can only rate each other if they were part of a *Verified* team (all members confirmed via UM-verified accounts). Ratings are **double-blind** and multi-dimensional, building a permanent, portable "Collaboration Score" that future teammates can trust.

## ✨ Key Features
*   **Skill-Forward Profiles:** LinkedIn-style profiles showcasing technical and soft skills.
*   **Team Verification:** No quorum, no credit. Teams are only official when every member confirms.
*   **Double-Blind Rating:** You cannot see your ratings until you submit yours.
*   **Multi-Dimensional Scoring:** Contribution, Reliability, Communication, Quality, Teamwork.
*   **Cold-Start Mitigation:** Newcomer cohort matching and first-team unlocks.
