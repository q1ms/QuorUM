01 — The Choice

Agile (Scrum)

Rather than a rigid Waterfall approach, Quorum will be developed using Agile Scrum — an iterative, incremental framework that delivers working software in short cycles.

Iterative

2-Week Sprints

Development is broken into time-boxed sprints. Each sprint delivers a testable, working increment of the system.

Incremental

Build, Test, Repeat

We build one feature at a time — starting with profiles and verification — test it with real users, then move to the next.

Collaborative

Continuous Feedback

Every sprint ends with a review and retrospective, allowing the team to adapt based on what actually works.

03 — The Phases

What Happens in a Sprint

01

Sprint Planning

The team selects the highest-priority items from the backlog. Example: Sprint 1 focuses only on student profiles and UM-verified login.

02

Design

UI/UX creates Figma wireframes. The System Analyst updates the ERD to match the sprint's scope.

03

Development

Programmers build the feature. The Network Engineer sets up the database tables and access controls.

04

Testing

QA runs functional tests. We conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with real students to validate the verification flow.

05

Review & Retrospective

The team reviews the working increment, updates the Gantt chart, and asks: What went well? What needs fixing? Then the next sprint begins.

04 — Justification

Why Agile?

Four reasons this methodology fits the Quorum project and our team's constraints.

↺

Changing Requirements

The double-blind rating rules may need tweaking after user testing. Agile lets us pivot without restarting the entire project.

⏱

Student Schedules

2-week sprints fit perfectly around our exams, assignments, and other course commitments. Waterfall's long phases don't.

◈

Prototype-Driven

The course requires a functional prototype. Agile guarantees we have a working system early, not just documents at the end.

⚠

Risk Management

We test the complex verification flow in Week 6, not Week 12. If something fails, we still have time to fix it.
