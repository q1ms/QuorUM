01 — The Problem

Two Failures in Group Formation

Every semester, students are forced to find group mates. This informal process is broken for two types of people.

The Lone Ranger

Left Out

Capable students with small networks get excluded because they don't have a big circle to ask. Group formation becomes a popularity contest, not a meritocracy.

The Free Rider

Carried Through

Someone does no work, but gets the same grade. No system records who actually contributed, so they do it again next semester to a different group.

The Root Cause

Information Asymmetry

Contribution is invisible before a project, and forgotten after it ends. So students default to whoever they already know.

02 — The Solution

Three Pillars of Quorum

Quorum is a LinkedIn-style platform for academic group projects, built on verified peer reviews.

Pillar 1 — Find

Skill-Forward Profiles

Students list their skills, availability, and portfolio links. Search and filter to find compatible teammates beyond your existing social circle.

Pillar 2 — Verify

The Quorum Check

When a team forms, every member must confirm via a UM-verified account. Only when all members confirm is the team marked Verified.

Pillar 3 — Rate

Double-Blind Reputation

After the project, members rate each other on 5 dimensions. You can't see your ratings until you submit yours. Builds a portable Collaboration Score.

03 — The Core Mechanic

What "Verified" Actually Means

Our verification layer is what makes Quorum different. No quorum, no credit.

V1 — Team registers with course code & semester
↓
V2 — Every member confirms via UM-verified account
↓
V3 — All members confirmed = bound to a real course
↓
V4 — Team status = VERIFIED. Rating unlocked at project end.
✓

Only Verified Teammates Can Rate

You cannot rate someone you didn't work with. This completely stops fake reviews and popularity gaming.

✓

Double-Blind & Delayed

Ratings open only after the project ends. You can't see your score until you submit yours. This prevents retaliation and petty ratings.

04 — Project Scope

What We Are Building

To ensure we deliver a high-quality prototype in one semester, we have strictly defined what is in and out of scope for Version 1.

IN

In Scope

Verified student profiles (skill declaration) · Team creation & all-member confirmation · Team verification logic · Double-blind five-dimension rating · Collaboration Score display · Browse/search/filter · Basic admin dashboard.

OUT

Out of Scope (Version 1)

UM SIS / MAYA integration · In-app chat · File submission & version control · AI-based matching (we use rule-based filtering) · Native mobile app · Multi-faculty rollout.
