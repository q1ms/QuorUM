02 — ISP Step 1

Current Situation

How group formation currently works in FCSIT.

Processes

Manual & Informal

Group formation is done via WhatsApp groups or verbal agreements. No centralized process exists.

Data

Scattered

Student skills, availability, and project histories are scattered across chats and personal spreadsheets. No record of contribution exists.

Technology

Consumer Apps

Reliance on WhatsApp, Telegram, and Google Forms. There is no academic-specific tooling.

03 — ISP Step 2

Future Situation

What FCSIT looks like with Quorum.

Processes

Verified & Automated

Students create skill-forward profiles, form verified teams, and exchange double-blind ratings after projects.

Data

Centralized

A dedicated database tracks verified student skills, team rosters, and permanent collaboration scores.

Human Resources

Merit-Based

Lone rangers compete on verified track records. Free riders are exposed by low contribution scores. Lecturers gain verifiable data.
